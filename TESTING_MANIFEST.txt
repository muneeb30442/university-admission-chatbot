RESPONSE GENERATION METHODS:

Rule-Based Keyword Matching:
  Accuracy:     65-75%
  Speed:        1-2ms (fastest)
  Memory:       Minimal
  Scalability:  Excellent
  Strengths:    Fast, simple, keyword-matching

Intent Classification (ML):
  Accuracy:     75-85%
  Speed:        5-10ms
  Memory:       ~50MB for models
  Scalability:  Good
  Strengths:    Recognizes intent variations
  Weaknesses:   May miss novel phrasings

NLP Similarity (TF-IDF):
  Accuracy:     70-80%
  Speed:        10-20ms
  Memory:       ~30MB
  Scalability:  Good
  Strengths:    Semantic understanding
  Weaknesses:   Slower processing

Ensemble (All Methods Combined):
  Accuracy:     80-90% ⭐ BEST
  Speed:        25-40ms
  Memory:       ~100MB combined
  Scalability:  Good
  Strengths:    Combines all strengths, highest accuracy
  Weaknesses:   Slower but most reliable
